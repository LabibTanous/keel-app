# Keel — Master Success List

**Governing rule: simplicity.** Keel wins by doing one job — income arrives, it splits, what's left is yours — better than anyone, and by refusing everything else.
Most items below are about NOT doing things: fewer features, fewer screens, fewer notifications, fewer decisions, built to a higher standard by one person.
When in doubt: copy before default, default before feature, decline before build.

---

## A. Positioning

**1. Pass the one-breath pitch test** — Lock "Money arrives, Keel splits it, what's left is yours to spend" as the landing headline, App Store subtitle, and bio; any feature that makes that sentence longer waits.

**2. Name the enemy: the lump** — The villain is one undifferentiated bank balance plus mental math ("stop guessing how much of that AED 14,500 invoice is actually yours"); the comparison page is Keel vs. your spreadsheet, never Keel vs. other fintechs.

**3. Lead with the outcome, not the mechanism** — Onboarding and marketing open with the smoothed paycheck number ("your steady monthly pay: AED 9,200"); the six pots appear one screen later as how it's achieved.

**4. Stay a wedge, refuse the platform** — Roadmap rule: if a feature doesn't touch the moment income arrives and splits, it's deferred by default — no invoicing, no cards, no banking, no expense analytics.

**5. Stay virtual forever** — Pots are labels on one balance, never real transfers; this kills licensing and compliance complexity and doubles as the marketing line.

**6. Own Zakat and AED as the moat** — Sequence and YNAB structurally can't serve GCC freelancers; make the Zakat pot and a 9% UAE corporate-tax preset first-class, and claim "built for UAE freelancers," never "also available in UAE."

**7. Turn no-bank-connection into the trust pitch** — "Works with any bank. We never move, hold, or see your money" — a claim ACH routers can't make, stated plainly on the landing page.

**8. Speak to one persona in one vocabulary** — Every string says invoice, client, and gig — never salary except the paycheck Keel manufactures — and screenshots show a USD invoice landing in an AED life.

**9. Publish a "what Keel won't do" page** — One static page of refusals (no yield, no crypto, no credit, not tax advice) with one line each; it markets the focus and doubles as the founder's scope-creep firewall.

**10. Run the DM pitch test before building more** — Send the hero sentence alone to 20 UAE freelancers on X/LinkedIn and count link requests; iterate the sentence, not the app, until roughly a third bite.

## B. First 60 Seconds

**11. The landing page is the product** — The hero is a working client-side split widget, and every shared link routes to a pre-seeded demo of an AED 12,000 invoice splitting live; "Keep these pots" carries the state into the anonymous session.

**12. Deliver the first split in under 60 seconds** — Screen one asks one number — "roughly what did you earn last month?" — and on submit immediately animates the split into default pots; no account, no email, no other questions.

**13. End session one on safe-to-spend** — Close onboarding on one big AED figure ("Yours to spend: AED 7,350") with pots stacked quietly beneath, and make that screen the app's home so session two opens on the same promise.

**14. Opinionated pots, taught by dragging** — Preload Bills/Tax/Zakat/Buffer/Goals/Spending with UAE-sensible percentages; draggable sliders with the safe-to-spend number updating live are the entire education layer — no pot builder, no tour.

**15. Delete the welcome carousel** — Zero intro slides, coach marks, or checklists; the income input is the onboarding.

**16. Stay anonymous until there's something to lose** — Persist the anonymous session so pots survive refresh and return visits, and never show an auth wall anywhere in the first-run path.

**17. Ask for signup with loss aversion, one field** — After the first real income or on the second visit, show a single email field — "Keep your pots safe on any device" — dismissible, shown at most twice, no password ceremony.

**18. Hide power features until their trigger** — FX appears only when a non-AED amount is entered, receipt scan after the first expense, the adviser after the first full split; the first run shows pots and nothing else.

**19. Empty states are the demo** — An empty Keel shows a ghosted sample month with "see how AED 12,000 splits," every element badged "Sample," replaced by real data on the first logged income.

**20. Aim session two at the next payday** — One optional question ("when's your next payment due?") and exactly one email that day ("Payment landed? Split it") — no reminder engine.

**21. Offer the iOS shortcut only after the aha** — "Add the 2-tap logging shortcut" is the single suggested next step on the safe-to-spend screen, never during onboarding itself.

## C. Habit & Retention

**22. The split is the only celebration** — When income lands: one push plus a 3-second pot-fill animation ending on "yours to spend: AED X" — and zero celebration anywhere else in the app.

**23. Three notifications, each complete** — Hard-code exactly income-received, smoothed-payday, and bill-due-soon, each containing the whole answer ("AED 8,200 → 5,740 set aside, 2,460 yours") so the loop closes from the lock screen; never "we miss you," tips, or streak nags.

**24. Buffer meter instead of streaks** — Show "Buffer covers 6 weeks of bills" — a number that dips but never resets to zero; no streaks, badges, or gamification anywhere, ever, because a freelancer's income gap breaking a streak converts calm into guilt.

**25. Design the overspend screen for a bad week** — When Spending runs out, offer a calm one-tap "top up from Buffer" rebalance — no red screens, no "over budget" language, framed as adjusting the split.

**26. Smoothed payday is a fixed monthly ritual** — On the 1st, the paycheck "arrives" in the Spending pot with its own quiet notification — the second, and only other, celebrated moment.

**27. One two-minute Friday digest** — Earned, split, safe-to-spend, one suggested tweak — deliberately finishable in under two minutes with nothing below to scroll.

**28. Let sessions end themselves** — Home is one card with no feed, no infinite transaction list, and nothing to pull-to-refresh for; a glance is enough by design.

**29. Never assign homework** — Splits stay automatic, scanning stays optional, and no badge, count, or inbox ever accumulates unfinished tasks.

**30. The adviser is pull-only** — It never initiates: no insight cards, no AI pushes; it answers only when opened with a question.

**31. Show runway, not emptiness** — In no-income weeks, home shows "Buffer and pots cover you to Sep 14" instead of zero-activity nags or grayed-out charts.

**32. Measure monthly split-witnesses, not DAU** — The one retention metric is the percentage of users who see at least one split per month; DAU stays low on purpose.

**33. Quiet mode before they uninstall** — One toggle silences everything except payday for 30 days while splits keep running silently — keeping the reinstall path warm.

**34. One calm Ramadan prompt** — Once before Ramadan: "Your Zakat pot has AED X set aside (estimate)" — and nothing more.

## D. Trust

**35. "Keel never moves your money" — verbatim, three places** — Put "Pots are labels, not transfers — Keel never touches your money" word-for-word identical on the landing hero, the first-run screen, and the pot-creation sheet.

**36. Anonymous-first is the privacy headline** — The landing's second line: "No account, no bank connection — try it with fake numbers first" — the architecture is the marketing.

**37. One consistent estimate tag** — A single ~ prefix plus muted "estimate" chip on every tax, Zakat, and paycheck figure, with a tap revealing the two or three assumptions in plain text.

**38. Show the math under every number** — "10,000 AED → 20% Tax 2,000 · 10% Zakat 1,000 · rest 7,000" as one plain text line; verifiable arithmetic substitutes for the brand trust a solo founder doesn't have yet.

**39. The founder is the social proof** — A signed "Why I built Keel" note with a real reply-to email, a weekly dated /changelog as proof of life, and first-person replies to every review and email within 24 hours; never a fabricated testimonial or user count.

**40. Disclaimers inline, in human words** — "A rough set-aside, not tax advice — confirm with your accountant or the FTA" as body text next to the number; delete any blocking legal modals.

**41. A one-page plain-English privacy note** — Exactly what's stored in Convex, what text goes to Anthropic and when, that nothing is sold, dated and signed by Labib.

**42. One-tap delete-everything, advertised** — A settings button that wipes the user's Convex records immediately, with "delete everything anytime, instantly" stated on the landing page.

**43. Answer "how do you make money?" unprompted** — One FAQ line: free while in beta, a simple paid subscription later, data never sold or used for ads.

**44. Treat Zakat with humility and user control** — "Your rate, your choice" with 2.5% as an editable default, one reputable source linked, and output never phrased as a religious ruling.

**45. Show FX rates with source and date** — The rate used and when it was fetched in small text under every converted figure (state the fixed 3.6725 USD-AED peg).

**46. Ask for reviews at the moment of relief** — The single native review prompt fires once, right after the third successful split, and nowhere else.

**47. Just-in-time permissions with a one-line reason** — Camera is requested only at the first receipt-scan tap, preceded by "used to read this receipt — photos aren't stored."

## E. Ruthless Simplicity

**48. Safe-to-spend is the entire home screen** — One big AED number with a thin pot strip below; charts, history, and adviser live one tap deeper, never on this screen.

**49. The sentence test gates every feature** — Before building anything, write it into "this makes my irregular income feel more like a salary" — if the sentence reads false, it goes to the backlog graveyard.

**50. Six pots, percentages only** — Renaming and percentage tweaks are allowed; creating, reordering, or proliferating custom pots is not, until real users demand it.

**51. Refuse budgets and expense categories outright** — Spending is one undifferentiated pot; "Keel splits income, it doesn't police spending" is copy on the pot screen, not a categorization engine.

**52. One smoothing rule, zero knobs** — Trailing 3-month median, floored conservatively, stated in one plain sentence under the paycheck number; refuse a smoothing-settings screen forever.

**53. Convert foreign income once, at entry** — USD/EUR/GBP/SAR converts to AED at log time, marked ≈ with the rate shown once; no FX dashboard, no rate alerts, no per-currency balances.

**54. Tax and Zakat are copy, not calculators** — Preset percentages with honest one-line explainers; neither ever grows input fields, because a solo-maintained tax engine is unbuildable and a well-worded default is shippable.

**55. Copy → default → decline → build** — Triage every request down that ladder; "I don't get the paycheck number" is fixed by renaming and one explainer line, never by a toggle.

**56. One short settings screen, forever** — Pot percentages, payday cadence, delete-my-data; anything else proposed as a setting becomes the default for everyone or doesn't exist.

**57. Keep a public NOT-BUILDING list** — In the repo and on the site: bank sync, bill pay, invoicing, subscription tracking, crypto, Android-native, team accounts — one line each on why; close any matching idea instantly, including your own at 2am.

**58. Income logging under ten seconds** — The iOS shortcut and quick-log form are the core product surface; measure seconds-to-log, polish those two flows relentlessly, and add nothing else to capture.

**59. Demote receipt scanning below income logging** — "Log income" is the only prominent add button; scanning lives behind the Spending pot — or gets cut from v1 entirely if OCR quality needs babysitting.

## F. Engineering Discipline

**60. Keep the split engine pure and deterministic** — `splitIncome(amountFils, rules) -> allocations` in /lib with zero imports from Convex, React, Date, or Math.random; mutations, previews, and the adviser all call that one function.

**61. Store money as integer minor units only** — Every amount in Convex is integer fils end-to-end; Intl.NumberFormat formats AED only at the render edge, because off-by-a-fils floats destroy trust instantly.

**62. Anchor money math with golden table tests** — ~40 Vitest cases covering rounding remainders (always assigned to Spending), exact-100% allocations, zero income, and each currency, run by the same gate as every deploy.

**63. Never lose an anonymous user's ledger** — One optional magic-link "claim your data" upgrade (no passwords, no OAuth matrix), and manually test the cleared-site-data path before each release.

**64. Fail quiet: retry once, roll back, calm toast** — One safeMutation wrapper handles retry, optimistic rollback, and "Couldn't sync — nothing was lost," logging to a Convex errors table reviewed weekly.

**65. Keep AI off the critical path** — The adviser lives on its own route with a hard monthly token budget and a graceful "adviser is resting" state; splits, pots, and the paycheck never touch the API.

**66. Snapshot the FX rate on every income row** — Store rate and rateDate at log time from a once-daily cached fetch with a hardcoded fallback table; never re-fetch rates for old rows, so history is always reproducible.

**67. Render last-known pots instantly, even offline** — Mirror the latest snapshot to localStorage and hydrate stale-while-revalidate; no service worker, no sync engine — the metro glance-check is the whole experience.

**68. Boring and light by budget** — Runtime deps under ~15 (Next.js, Convex, Tailwind, Anthropic SDK, Zod), no state/ORM/chart/date libraries; server components with one client island, CSS/SVG charts, first-load JS eyeballed under ~150 KB each release.

**69. One command gates one pipeline** — `npm run check` (typecheck + test + lint) is the only gate; PRs get Vercel previews, main auto-deploys prod, and there is no staging or feature-flag system.

**70. Derive balances; replay history, never mutate it** — Paycheck and pot balances compute on read from raw income rows using versioned split rules (old rows replay under their rule version); Convex schema stays additive-only.

**71. Make the shortcut endpoint idempotent** — POST /api/log requires a client-generated idempotency key plus a bearer token, and returns the resulting split as read-back confirmation — one double-logged invoice corrupts every downstream split.

**72. Count five events, skip analytics SaaS** — landed, income_logged, split_viewed, returned_7d, adviser_used in one Convex table, read as a single weekly funnel query — nothing else.

## G. AI That Earns Its Place

**73. AI never touches balances** — Splits, smoothing, and pot balances are pure Convex functions; the Anthropic layer only reads their outputs and can never write or compute a number the user sees.

**74. Spend the AI budget on input friction only** — Receipt and bank-SMS parsing into a pre-filled transaction is the flagship AI feature; the adviser stays a thin secondary surface, and nothing else AI-shaped gets built.

**75. Every extraction is an editable draft** — A scanned receipt opens a pre-filled amount/currency/pot form with one Accept tap; nothing writes to Convex until confirmed, and low-confidence parses pre-fill the manual form instead of saving.

**76. Manual entry stays one tap from every AI path** — A parse failure or ~5-second timeout drops straight into the quick-log form with whatever was extracted already pre-filled; an Anthropic outage must never block logging money.

**77. Hard-scope the adviser: no picks, no rulings** — System prompt plus a cheap output check refuse buy/sell and tax/Zakat-ruling asks with a canned reply that redirects to the user's own buffer math.

**78. Ground answers in real pot data** — Read-only tool access to pots and transactions, instructed to say "I can't see that" rather than infer any number it didn't fetch, with a visible "AI — can be wrong" label on replies.

**79. The split never waits on AI** — Income splits instantly on fixed percentage rules; AI categorization and merchant cleanup arrive seconds later as a quiet, dismissible suggestion.

**80. Cap AI cost per device before shipping it** — Convex-side daily caps (e.g., 20 scans + 10 adviser messages per anonymous device), Haiku-class models for parsing, prompt caching — anonymous-first plus zero budget means one scripted abuser equals a month's bill.

**81. Never let the model guess FX** — AI extracts only the currency code and amount; conversion always uses the one pinned daily-rate source in code.

**82. Treat prompts as code with a fixed eval set** — Prompts live as TypeScript constants changed only via commits that run ~30 real UAE receipt fixtures (Arabic, VAT lines, SAR) in CI; log every user correction (original vs. edited) and skim it weekly to patch prompts — no ML pipeline, just reading.

**83. Ship one AI surface well, then freeze** — Get receipt parsing boringly accurate on real UAE receipts first; the adviser stays feature-frozen until draft-acceptance is high enough that users stop editing fields.

**84. Make the AI invisible** — Features are named "Scan receipt" and "Ask Keel" — no sparkle icons, no chatbot on home, AI mentioned only in the privacy note; calm-money users want less noise, not "AI-powered."

## H. Zero-Budget Growth

**85. Ride the UAE corporate-tax news cycle** — One evergreen "UAE freelancer tax deadlines" page, updated within 24 hours of every FTA announcement or penalty story, reposted into the same 3-4 groups each cycle, closing with "Keel sets the estimate aside automatically."

**86. Ship calculator pages, not a blog** — Exactly three static pages — corporate-tax set-aside calculator, AED 375k VAT-threshold tracker, Zakat estimator — using real UAE thresholds and AED figures (never $ placeholders), each ending "save this as a pot in Keel."

**87. Camp in three communities, not ten channels** — r/dubai + r/UAE, the two largest UAE-freelancer Facebook groups, and one coworking WhatsApp; answer tax-set-aside questions weekly with calculator links and never cold-post the app.

**88. Hand-onboard the first 20 users in person** — Two weeks of day-passes at Letswork/Nook/AstroLabs-style coworking spaces until 20 freelancers have split a real income payment in Keel; that's where the first testimonials and honest objections come from.

**89. Own "Sequence alternative UAE" queries** — 4-5 short, honest comparison pages ("Sequence alternative that works in AED," "YNAB for UAE freelancers") that concede what each rival does better, in the no-hype voice.

**90. Make the split card the entire referral loop** — One "share my split" image export (pot percentages, amounts optional, quiet wordmark) plus one 15-second screen-record of a payment splitting, reused across X, LinkedIn, TikTok, and the landing page — no referral codes, no rewards engine.

**91. Pitch creators one ready-made storyline** — DM five UAE money micro-creators (5-50k followers) with "I put myself on a salary" and barter free-forever access for one honest demo video — zero cash.

**92. Own Zakat SEO before Ramadan** — Publish the accurate, estimate-marked Zakat explainer and calculator now so it has ranked by February 2027; it's the one angle US competitors structurally can't copy.

**93. Catch freelancers at the visa-and-license moment** — One "just got your UAE freelance visa — money setup in 20 minutes" checklist page targeting those queries, linked whenever the question appears in groups.

**94. Skip the native app; distribute the Shortcut** — Publish the quick-log iOS Shortcut with a 60-second install guide plus PWA add-to-home-screen; list it on Shortcut galleries and r/shortcuts, and revisit ASO only after web traction proves demand.

**95. Track one metric; cull the weakest channel monthly** — UTM-tag every link, measure week-2 returning users per channel in Convex/Vercel analytics, and once a month kill the weakest activity and double the strongest.

**96. Delay Arabic until Saudi signups pull it** — English-only app, at most one Arabic Zakat/tax landing page for search; a cluster of SAR-income signups is the trigger to localize.

**97. Build in public on LinkedIn** — One post a week showing a real Keel screen with AED numbers and one honest lesson — Dubai is a LinkedIn-first city and consultants are the exact ICP; no growth-bro threads.

## I. Money Later

**98. Don't charge anyone yet — write the trigger** — Zero billing code before a written milestone (e.g., 100 users with 3+ splits who returned in week 4); at ~50 actives, run a fake-door "Keel Pro — AED 19/mo" page with a notify-me button and only build billing if taps justify it.

**99. Never paywall the split itself** — Unlimited pots, unlimited splits, and AED income tracking are free forever, in writing; any future Pro sits on top of the split, never gates it.

**100. Zakat, tax pots, and honesty features stay free** — A named, permanent commitment on the pricing page; paywalling a religious obligation or the safety rails would be the screenshot that ends the app.

**101. Make free complete, make Pro a growth milestone** — Free covers the whole loop for a single-currency AED freelancer; Pro = multi-currency smoothing, longer history, deeper AI — things you need because your freelance business grew.

**102. One flat AED price, one tier, no meters** — Roughly AED 19/month or AED 180/year, describable in one sentence, with no add-ons, seats, or overage charges ever.

**103. Meter only the AI adviser, and say why** — A small free monthly allowance with the literal line "AI answers cost us money — Pro removes the cap," matching the honest voice.

**104. Prompt upgrades at payday, never at broke moments** — The only upgrade surface is the post-split confirmation ("Keel just set aside AED 2,140 for tax and bills"); zero upsells on low-balance, overspend, or empty states.

**105. Let the counter justify the price** — A lifetime "Keel has set aside AED X for you" tally placed next to the AED 19 price on the upgrade screen — the app's own running total dwarfing the subscription.

**106. Sell a capped Founding Member deal, grandfather it publicly** — Exactly 100 spots at one-time ~AED 299 with a permanent badge, closed publicly and forever; every early subscriber keeps their signup price for life, and the pricing page says so.

**107. Put "you pay us or nobody does" on the pricing page** — One sentence — no ads, data never sold, funded by subscriptions only — held to in every future decision.

**108. Never hold the user's data hostage** — CSV export of all transactions and pot history stays free and one tap away, including for cancelled and anonymous users.

**109. Merchant of record, zero billing infra** — Paddle/Lemon Squeezy with two SKUs handling GCC cards, taxes, and invoices; receipts carry the user's business name so "expense it — Keel is a business tool."

**110. Payment is the only step that requires signup** — Free users run splits, pots, and exports fully anonymous forever; email is requested exactly once, at checkout, framed as needed for the receipt.

## J. Psychology That Makes It Work

**111. Split income before the user sees it whole** — The split receipt shows first and the gross amount never appears as an "available balance" anywhere — once money is seen whole, every dirham feels spendable.

**112. Smooth every windfall into the paycheck** — Default the steady paycheck to ~80% of trailing 3-month median, labeled estimate, with the surplus auto-flowing to Buffer — fixing fat-month overspend and lean-month panic at once.

**113. Route big invoices through the exact same split** — No size threshold or special casing: a 30k AED project payment splits by identical rules, because windfall "play money" is exactly how freelancers blow up.

**114. Never moralize spending — celebrate it** — Spending is never red or "over budget"; the voice says "enjoy it — bills are already handled," because permission to spend is the retention loop and shame is the churn loop.

**115. Friction out, none in** — Splitting in is fully automatic; moving money out of a pot triggers exactly one consequence-framed confirm ("Bills pot will be AED 900 short on Aug 1") — no lectures.

**116. Hide the aggregate balance** — Pots are the default view; the combined total requires a deliberate tap and is framed as "mostly spoken for," not wealth — one pooled number makes money fungible again.

**117. Limit money decisions to one monthly ritual** — A single 5-minute review ("adjust paycheck? tweak percentages?") is the only place adjustments happen; Keel's promise is fewer money decisions, not another dashboard.

**118. Ban urgency and hype language everywhere** — Marked estimates and no-sell copy reduce reactance and build compliance; audit the app and marketing for urgency words and delete them.

**119. End every session at peace** — Peak-end rule: whatever the user did, the final screen is the safe-to-spend number, so the memory Keel leaves is always calm.

**120. One goal at a time** — The Goals pot holds a single named goal with a simple progress bar — goal-gradient motivation without ever growing into a goals dashboard.
